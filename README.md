# Customer Shopping Behaviour | Microsoft Excel

## 🎯 Project Objective
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of this interactive dashboard is to analyze customer shopping behavior and uncover meaningful patterns in purchasing habits, product preferences, brand choices, regional trends, payment preferences, and customer spending. It provides interactive insights into purchase frequency, order value, quantity purchased, discounts, returns, and profitability to help businesses understand customer needs, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer shopping and transaction-level information used to analyze purchasing behavior, product preferences, regional performance, payment methods, discounts, returns.
The dataset includes key attributes such as customer details, purchase information, product and brand details, region, transaction dates, quantity, payment mode, discounts, delivery information, and return status.

### Dataset Columns
Cloumn	Description
Transaction ID	Unique identifier for each transaction
Customer ID	Unique identifier for each customer
Purchase Date	Date on which the purchase was made
Month	Month in which the purchase was made
Year	Year in which the purchase was made
Quarters	Quarter in which the purchase was made
Age	Customer's age at the time of purchase
Customer Age Group	Age range assigned based on the customer's age
Gender	Gender category of the customer
Location	Geographical location of the customer or purchase
Region	Broader geographical region of the customer or purchase
Online/Offline	Sales channel used for the purchase
Online Store	Online platform used for the purchase
Category	Product category of the purchase
Item Purchased	Specific product purchased
Brand	Brand or manufacturer of the product
Colour	Colour selected for the product
Size	Size selected for the product
Quantity	Number of units purchased
Purchase Amount	Total amount spent on the purchase
Discount (%)	Percentage discount applied to the purchase
